# Background and Similar Applications

## Introduction

Before defining Bella Dona ERP, we reviewed some applications that are already used for business management.

The purpose of this comparison was to identify the main functions offered by these applications and find ideas that could be useful for our project.

The applications we reviewed were:

- Treinta.
- Odoo Community.
- Zoho Inventory.
- Dolibarr ERP.



## Comparative Table

| Application | Cost | Suppliers | Users / HR | Inventory / Warehouse | Sales / Billing | Customers / CRM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Treinta | Free basic plan | Basic contact management | Single-user access | Basic stock control | Sales registration | Basic customer records |
| Odoo Community | Free / Open Source | Supplier and purchasing management | Employee and user management | Multi-warehouse and stock control | Point of Sale and sales management | Customer follow-up |
| Zoho Inventory | Freemium | Purchase order management | Limited users depending on plan | Stock and product tracking | Basic invoicing | Customer information |
| Dolibarr ERP | Free / Self-Hosted | Supplier and purchasing management | Employee management | Stock management | Sales and Point of Sale | Customer and prospect management |



## Treinta

Treinta is an application focused on helping small businesses manage their daily sales and business information.

Some of its functions include:

- Sales registration.
- Basic inventory control.
- Customer information.
- Mobile access.

We considered Treinta as a reference because it focuses on small businesses and daily commercial activities.



## Odoo Community

Odoo Community is an open-source ERP that has different modules for business management.

Some of its functions include:

- Supplier management.
- Purchasing.
- Inventory.
- Sales.
- Point of Sale.
- Customer management.
- Employee management.

Odoo is a complete ERP, but our project will be more focused on the specific needs of Bella Dona.



## Zoho Inventory

Zoho Inventory is mainly focused on inventory and sales management.

It includes functions such as:

- Product management.
- Inventory tracking.
- Purchase orders.
- Invoicing.
- Customer information.

Some functions depend on the plan being used.



## Dolibarr ERP

Dolibarr is an open-source ERP and CRM platform.

It includes:

- Supplier management.
- Purchasing.
- Inventory.
- Sales.
- Employee management.
- Customer management.

It is useful as a reference because it shows how different business processes can be managed in one system.



## What We Identified

After reviewing these applications, we identified some functions that could be useful for Bella Dona ERP.

However, Bella Dona has specific characteristics because it sells gothic necklaces and accessories.

For this reason, our system could include product information related to:

- Materials.
- Chain types.
- Charms.
- Gothic styles.
- Product images.

We also plan to include inventory alerts and basic information about sales and customers.



## Value Added

The main value added by Bella Dona ERP will be its focus on the needs of Bella Dona.

Instead of creating a general ERP with many functions, we want to focus on the processes that are most important for this business.



## Conclusion

The applications reviewed provide different useful functions for business management.

Our project will use some of these ideas as a reference, but Bella Dona ERP will be designed according to the needs of the Bella Dona business and its gothic product style.
